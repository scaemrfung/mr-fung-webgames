How to play

  1. Put in Order: tap the mixed-up letters, first in the alphabet to last, to fill the row.
  2. Missing Letter: look at the row, work out which letter is missing, and tap it.
  3. Tap a letter you placed to send it back. A star is earned for every round you finish — solve it on your first try to build a streak.

Tip: say each letter out loud as you tap it. The yellow A–Z strip can help.
