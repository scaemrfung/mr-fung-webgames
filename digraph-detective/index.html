Detective notes

  • A digraph is two letters that work together to make one sound — like ch in chair.
  • wh starts words like whale and wheel. It does not end words, so it never appears in the “Ends with” game.
  • Listen for the sound at the very start or the very end of the word — the middle letters are not the clue.
