Ready when you are
Say it like this:
Ta

One quarter note. It lasts the whole beat — say “Ta” and clap once.

Ti-Ti

Two eighth notes sharing one beat. Say “Ti-Ti” and clap twice, quickly.

Rest

A silent beat — a real quarter rest. Keep the beat in your head, but make no sound.

1

Pick a beat

2

Press Play and just listen once — hands still for the rest. Point to each beat as it sounds.

3

Clap & say it

Turn Loop on, say “Ta, Ti-Ti, Ta” out loud, and clap along as a class.
