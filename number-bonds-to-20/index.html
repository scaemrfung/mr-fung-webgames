How to play

  1. Look at the frame. The filled dots are the number you already have.
  2. Count the empty spaces. That is how many more you need.
  3. Tap the missing number. A wrong tap is okay — just try again. A star is earned when you get it right the first time.
  4. Say the bond out loud: “7 and 3 make 10.” Saying it helps you remember it!
